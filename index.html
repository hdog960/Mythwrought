MYTHWROUGHT

Engineering prototype: Move with the left pad, look with the right pad, and MINE/PLACE the block in the center of your view. JUMP from the left grip.

Desktop: WASD move, arrow keys look, Space jump; click mines, right-click places, mouse drag looks.

Both controller grips resize together. The 3D world remains fully visible between them.

Swipe directly or hold the right pad to turn. Hold AIM or Shift for fine adjustments.

Different seeds have separate saves; older prototype worlds remain available.

This early prototype uses browser local storage. Export world edits regularly; a production-safe IndexedDB save system is not yet implemented.
