Stone & Nature: Flowers and Mushrooms

ISOLATED CANDIDATE GALLERY — NOT MERGED / NOT DEPLOYED. This is a Canvas2D preview of physical asset descriptors, not the live Mythwrought game.

Four distinct flowers and three different mushrooms, each individually placeable, targetable and removable as one test voxel. All plant parts have real geometry; even passable petals and hollow spaces are represented accurately. The pearl mushroom has pale visual art, not functional light emission.

Cross-section at specimen-specific height: golden = solid targetable geometry; dark = true empty space. Each preview is derived from actual bounding boxes. Rotated fixtures deliberately cannot save until the central engine has safe orientation persistence.
