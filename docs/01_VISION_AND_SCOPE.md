# Authority 01 — Vision, non-negotiables and scope
**Status:** canonical design authority, 2026-11-10. This document describes the target game, not existing code.

## Game identity
**MYTHWROUGHT — A world to shape. A story to change.** Original first-person fantasy RPG for landscape iPhone, installed from GitHub Pages. Inspiration DNA: Minecraft's unrestricted voxel world, Skyrim's real-time hands/weapons/spells/navigation, Baldur's Gate 3's meaningful authored relationships/choices; underground visual feeling combines eerie inhabited Underdark, colossal dark Depths and modern Minecraft cave geometry. Original characters, lore, creatures and assets—not licensed designs.

## Non-negotiable player promises
- One enormous seamless world. Surface, caves, dungeons and subterranean civilizations occupy the SAME coordinates and editable voxel data; no separate underground dimension or transition rooms.
- Procedural seeded geography, biome/depth ores and caves; mandatory authored main-story cities and landmarks placed deterministically with validated approaches, accessibility and quest dependencies.
- Every ordinary structural block can be placed/removed. Floating castles and unsupported cliffs remain: NO load-bearing collapse, except configured falling substances such as gravel/sand.
- Rich block building, modular fantasy roofing/arches/beams/pillars/furniture, blueprint capture AND full virtual 3D blueprint editing; never overwrite world edits without consent.
- Classless custom hero. Ancestry and roleplaying background affect starting proficiencies/possessions/dialogue, not long-term access to skills or moral alignment.
- Real-time responsive, weighty, straightforward combat; equipment combinations such as sword+shield, sword+spell, dual weapons and bow; readable tells, no Soulslike parry dependency.
- Two active authored companions maximum, deep **platonic** relationships, contextual dialogue and individual quests. They are mostly reactive, never independently alter blocks, consume rare resources or decide irreversible events.
- One substantial authored overarching storyline (less sprawling than BG3), regional consequences and several meaningful faction/finale choices. Ordinary building/exploration/settlement play never gated behind completing the plot.
- Safe ordinary magic, combinable essence/form/modifier spellcraft. Forbidden ancient magic is exceptional with clearly previewed narrative/ethical/environmental stakes.
- A positive, colorful high-fantasy surface paired with pitch-black expanses, luminous wilds and deeply inhabited subterranean cities.
- Landscape **console-like physical side grips**: left movement, right look/actions, compass in top frame; central first-person view completely free of buttons/HUD.
- Responsive tactile art style: premium handcrafted pixel-textured voxels, expressive stylized characters, warm architecture, atmospheric lights. Quality must be proven, never excused by mobile constraints.
- GitHub Pages/static only and no recurring paid dependencies.

## Player lives supported
Adventurer and storyteller; mage and spelunker; blacksmith and architect; enchanted factory inventor; farmer/fisher; cave-city resident; sanctuary naturalist; builder of roads, trade routes, towns and underground estates. These share one coherent world and character. Most life systems are optional, not punitive chores.

## Explicit boundaries to prevent runaway scope
- No mandatory hunger or weather maintenance, permadeath, Soulslike combat, romance, universal NPC career trees, gigantic industrial automation simulation or expensive geological simulation.
- No routine item loss beyond a recoverable Death Echo.
- No mandatory creature collection or forced wildlife micromanagement.
- No real-time simulation of the entire procedural world while distant/unloaded.
- No artificial path-blocking or unbreakable blanket story terrain where resilient authored quest logic can serve.
- Avoid spending art effort on throwaway effects; original materials, silhouettes, animation and lighting quality first.
- Ambition is **end-state aspiration**. Features are added behind tests and visual gates; never claim AAA size or performance in advance.

## Narrative/gameplay scale
One strong authored campaign with four broad narrative movements, branching regional choices, story-rich party NPCs, personal questlines and optional content. Not BG3's cutscene/branch count. Initial playable vertical slice is intentionally small and not a substitute for the full vision.

## Release governance
Seven docs here define the contract. Resolve contradictory docs in sequence: explicit later user approval > this vision authority > relevant domain authority > roadmap > implementation notes. Update authority docs alongside deliberate scope changes. Release ledger records actual passing tests, commit and deployment results; never infer “implemented” from a roadmap entry. User retains approval for artistic and major design gates.
