## Frozen regional requirements

### D0

**The Monumental Foundation**

Complete physical monumental geometry, deterministic landmark ownership, shared-height integration, current-game rendering and persistent world identity.

Prioritize existing Phase 0 blockers over additional speculative landmark searches.

### D1

**The Golden Sands Restoration**

- Remove broad naturally generated white sand fields.
- Preserve only exceptionally small suitable pale salt patches.
- Introduce beautiful gold, amber, orange and red desert terrain.
- Improve natural transitions among sand, sandstone, hardpan and exposed rock.
- Preserve existing saves and materials.

### D2

**Giants of the Desert**

Develop juvenile, mature, ancient and twisted elder cactus families.

Preserve recognizable Mythwrought cactus silhouettes.

Mature cacti should commonly reach approximately 5–10 blocks where appropriate. Ancient specimens should reach the approved 12–18-block range, with distinctive branching variations.

Generate sparse groves and isolated giants.

### D3

**Landscapes of the Forgotten Sands**

Develop:
- Vast dune seas.
- Wind-carved sandstone.
- Cliffs.
- Canyons.
- Mesas.
- Natural arches.
- Dry basins.
- Dramatic rocky formations.
- Believable elevation transitions.

### D4

**Ancient Landmark Generation System**

Complete deterministic, rarity-aware, multichunk placement of landmarks.

Protect caves, terrain stability, walking routes, player edits and adjacent features.

### D5

**The Buried Kingdom**

Develop monumental block-built pyramids, lost temples, buried chambers and the Twin-Maw Sentinel.

The guardian must possess the approved double-headed serpent identity.

Include genuine explorable interiors where specified.

### D6

**Remains of the Impossible**

Develop enormous fossilized Worldwyrm or dragon skeletons, ancient fallen stone giants and other approved colossal remains.

They must be actual physical features, not scenery images.

### D7

**The Glass Scar and Flaming Wastes**

Develop naturally fused glass terrain, supernatural scorched landscapes and visible environmental flames.

Preserve traversal and use bounded effects.

Do not introduce uncontrolled destructive fire simulation.

### D8

**Monuments of the Three Moons**

Preserve the existing three-moon sky system.

### D9

**A Thousand Forgotten Wonders**

- Lost oases.

**Ancient World Distribution and Discovery**
