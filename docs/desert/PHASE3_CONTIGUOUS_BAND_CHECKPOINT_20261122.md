## Engineering decision
**Do not integrate this one-cell existing-rock apron into gameplay yet.** Its source tests are correct, but the outcome replaces one sharp 258/401 seam with a two-stage 258/261 and 261/401 seam, increasing total categorical boundaries by 36.8%. The original rejected sparse-hash candidate likewise failed human visual inspection. As both strategies only replace cube materials, source-level counts do not prove the new band attractive. A fresh broad Chromium comparison would be poor CI use before a plausible material-blending treatment exists. Therefore the optional WebGL2 comparison is **not run for this rejected-at-source-only proposal** and no screenshot/effect is claimed.
