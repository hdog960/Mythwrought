## Decision

**Retain as isolated research prototype, not approved to merge or deploy.** A color-only visual seam gradient appears feasible within the original M7 nine-float mesh contract and produces small real-game changes at both source-authentic junctions. It does **not** by itself resolve the broad noticeable border, and the original performance risk was guarded by later visibility-gated source code. Remaining gates: a wider/more coherent gradient evaluated close to actual seam witnesses, exact source-edit/cave masking, same-camera original-game screenshots for any materially new implementation, physical iPhone frame-time/GPU upload measurements and explicit user authorization before integration. Protected original gameplay `2bd0395...`, production, PRs, other biome/cave source, saves, Codex/automations, shared workflows and deployment remain unchanged.
