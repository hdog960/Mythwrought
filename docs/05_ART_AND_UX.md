# Authority 05 — Art quality, iPhone controls and accessibility
**Status:** aesthetic/interaction authority.

## Art vision
Premium original stylized fantasy VOXEL RPG. Coherent handcrafted pixel-textured block materials, carved stone, timber grain, roofs/arches/pillars, varied warm architecture, vegetation and distinctive culture-specific settlements. NO unstyled primitives left as final art. Characters are appealing expressive stylized voxel models with distinct ancestry silhouettes, readable gestures, layered outfits, facial expression and animated gait; do not allow mismatched sprite/model scales or placeholder humans in premium art gates. Visual fidelity comes from design/textures/animation first, computationally expensive shaders second.

## Palette / atmosphere
Surface: welcoming rich high fantasy, sunlight, color and inviting settlements with darker pockets. Underground: striking contrast between near-pitch-black immense depths, luminous fungus/crystals/lakes, and dense warm cities; original artistic designs, not replicas of copyrighted franchises. Visible silhouettes, honest darkness balanced with accessible immediate hazard visibility. Magic: distinct elemental color languages and readable environment effects, ordinary vs forbidden ancient magic visually differentiated. Death Echo: floating dark-centered violet/cyan/silver rift, clear light/sound and instantaneous collapse/recovery, no tombstone.

## Concept-art discussion — whimsical high fantasy direction (2026-11-11, NOT art approval)
The player shared ten aspirational Mythwrought concept images showing lively fantasy settlements, ancient bridges and towers, towering waterfall valleys, a warm bustling harbor, flowering and snow-covered biomes, an illuminated deep-city cavern, a moonlit magical sanctuary, a first-person view, and a colossal crystal-powered boss encounter. **These are mood/visual-direction references, not shipped game assets, literal build specifications, a promise of matching screenshot fidelity, or acceptance of the current prototype.** The actual iPhone game may be substantially *blockier*. The user explicitly likes the **whimsy**; lean confidently into that rather than muting the palette into gritty realism.

### Desired artistic feeling
- **Whimsical, vibrant, inviting high fantasy** with genuine grandeur, mystery and danger; charming, not childish. The world should invite lingering, exploration, settlement-building and discovery while allowing emotionally serious stories and frightening enemies.
- **Distinctive warm life at human scale:** cozy timber-and-stone houses, markets, lanterns, flags, gardens, flowers, pets/wildlife, lived-in streets and welcoming interiors. Make the ordinary world appealing, not only rare magical landmarks.
- **Epic depth and silhouettes:** massive bridges/aqueducts, layered castles, cliffside settlements, visible distant ruins, cascading waterfalls, monumental trees and recognizable skyline landmarks. Composition, verticality and readable landmarks convey wonder more reliably than polygon count.
- **Magic as a coherent visual language:** radiant crystal formations, glowing fungi, rune-work, ancient rings and strange luminous water against warmer lantern-lit civilization. Use magical hues meaningfully, not indiscriminate neon covering every surface.
- **Underground as a destination:** deep spaces can hold huge inhabited cities with rich warm light, water, greenery, architecture and a sense of home, as well as near-black perilous wilds. Do not reduce the underground to plain caves or uniform glowing mushrooms.
- **Lovely contrasts:** sunlit playful villages, blue-hour harbors, winter wonderlands, moonlit sanctuaries and threatening ancient bosses belong to the same cohesive game. Darkness and terror should have room to breathe without turning the whole setting bleak.
- **Expressive stylized inhabitants:** readable, personable voxel character silhouettes, characterful outfits/animation and distinctive creatures. The first-person player hands, tools, weapons and magic should eventually match the world style, without breaking the separate side-grip UI contract.

### Translation into a real editable iPhone voxel world
- **Art direction first; image fidelity second.** Achieve the feeling using original handcrafted material palettes, block-based architecture, disciplined lighting, vegetation shapes, strong color grouping and distance composition. Geometry and animations may be simplified to meet measured hardware budgets.
- Preserve recognizable block structure and broad editable world freedom. Avoid elaborate, uneditable scenery pasted over basic blocks or requiring exact image reproduction in an infinite procedural landscape. Large authored landmarks and settlements must integrate into deterministic seeded geography and the continuous editable volume.
- Balance visual richness against actual landscape-screen readability: navigable paths, visible hazards/telegraphs, useful contrast, reachable buildings and an unobstructed central 3D viewport. Sky, foliage and water need to remain readable at mobile resolution; keep a coherent style across all biomes instead of unrelated asset styles.
- The concept pieces are **a north star for atmosphere, composition and emotional response**, not a requirement that every settlement be an enormous palace or that every scene be saturated to its maximum. Keep resting places, humble buildings, wilderness and ordinary life alongside exceptional discoveries.
- **Approval remains open.** No real iPhone screenshot review, premium artwork signoff or Gate A completion is implied by this discussion. The next art review should show actual in-engine screenshots (surface, inhabited underground, dark wilderness and a readable character/first-person scene), comparing vibe rather than expecting concept-render equivalence. No new milestone or version advance is authorized by these notes.

## Landscape handheld controls — hard layout gate
Phone held LANDSCAPE. Decorated, integrated **left and right controller grips** akin to a fantasy handheld console. LEFT houses movement. RIGHT houses look/aim, attack, block/secondary action, interactions. Compass integrated into TOP CONTROLLER FRAME. Health/magic/equipment indicators in FRAME, not floating over 3D world. Central WORLD VIEWPORT MUST REMAIN ENTIRELY unobstructed by all touch controls, map pins, floating buttons, banners or HUD. Menu interfaces may occupy screen only when deliberately opened. Genuine comfortable two-thumb layout, safe areas/notches, ergonomic targets, drag-free right aiming, responsive input priority and no page scrolling/zoom gestures interfering.

## Visual acceptance
Before content scaling, test side-by-side screenshots of surface and deep caves on real landscape iPhone. Verify hand-made material richness, landmark silhouettes, creature/character scale, readable telegraphs, motion quality, dynamic readability, camera comfort and central viewport unobstructed. Artistic gates require explicit user acceptance. “Pretty enough” is not implied by test pass. Set budgets for draw calls, triangles, pixel ratio, texture memory after profiling actual hardware, not fabricated absolute promises.

## Accessibility
Brightness/underground visibility without punitive gameplay, color-independent cues, legible scaled text, reduced motion/VFX, touch size settings, remappable control placement within side grips, separate camera sensitivity, audio/vibration toggles, contrast/readability, optional hunger/weather, adjustable difficulty and safe-structure damage. Standalone Home Screen orientation should be tested, not assumed lockable in every browser. Do not overpromise offline if service worker is not installed/tested.
