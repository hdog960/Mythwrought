## Engineering design and limitations

1. Original roof: exactly 115,894 authored Y7 `live:wood` weather-plane cells, not Pack3. Exact 107,819 Y2–Y6 empty columns have authored navigation. Grounded full-height interior piers = zero. Original D19+D22+D25 Y6 candidates total 224 foundation-to-foundation spans, 1,698 provisional beam cells. Remaining radius-8 roof distance = 59,412 cells in 34 disconnected far zones. Proximity is **not a gravity model**.
2. D28 tests for longer original-source **straight x/z wall-to-wall corridors**, 13–48 unobstructed Y6 cells beneath continuous untouched Y7 `live:wood` roof. Each end must already have genuine Y0 foundation, Y1 floor and rooted Y2–Y6 wall/partition/column. Source candidates must avoid existing Y6 material, D19/D22/D25 proposed cells, and one another. No construction is performed. Source morphology may produce zero such spans; that's an engineering result, not a reason to fabricate one.
3. Original Pack2 ID61/ID62 composite face geometry is checked by exact union-area against the ORIGINAL shape owner; adjacent same-axis local-cell face contact must be 0.36 block². Genuine geometric contact does **not** impart moment stiffness or rated strength to one-cell timber joints. An end-to-end line of Pack2 solids is not a certified long timber beam or a real triangulated truss. Reject any automatic installation of these D28 design candidates.
4. D28 computes **ideal, uniform-line-load, simply supported *unit-demand* algebra** for an UNRATED continuous member: `R=qL/2`, `M=qL²/8`, `deflection=5qL⁴/(384EI)` with L measured from original wall-bearing cell centers. We do **not** know actual roof tributary width, `q`, timber strength, allowable deflection, effective `EI`, joint moment resistance, shear resistance, uplift, lateral buckling, foundation bearing or snow/wind load. A 28-cell clear span requires 4× the peak bending moment and 16× the deflection coefficient of a 14-cell clear span at the same unit line load. This is a demand illustration **not** a working stress or safe load certification.
5. Candidate ranking favors deep historical source-distance zones, not construction. To avoid false roof coverage credit, D28 optionally adds **one unapproved midpoint seed per geometrically intact long candidate** to the previous original source-only BFS; it labels resulting reductions *purely hypothetical*. Actual certified roof support remains unchanged. D28 explicitly records Y8/Y9 occupancies that block a naive roof-top parallel chord or exterior superstructure.
6. A future architecturally credible roof-wide strategy would require **structural redesign**, e.g. genuinely engineered exterior/perimeter bearing anchors with a mechanically rated truss or a source-compatible vaulted space-frame, actual transverse roof diaphragm transfer, tested load paths, and a collision-aware, editable physical block implementation. Exterior supports cannot be called grounded merely by drawing posts outside the authored footprint; their new foundations/loads require separately approved site engineering. Interior Y2 WALK routes cannot be changed. No structure has been built or approved in this lane.
