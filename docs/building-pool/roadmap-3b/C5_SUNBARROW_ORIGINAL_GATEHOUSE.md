## Original architecture and measured geometry

Sunbarrow is an independent irregular **39×34×17** envelope, **3,535 literal XYZ occupied source voxels**, **881 actual foundation columns**, and two truly absent-ground 3×3 cooling wells (18 completely empty columns). A **three-cell-wide, three-high, 34-cell-long** north-to-south wagon gateway spans the entire envelope with uninterrupted physical foundation and walking floor. Real four-high masonry posts flank the road at the source midpoint, under a source-backed three-wide y5 lintel. Source-gated passages link two uneven bastion wings, customs and arms rooms, rear supply and outpost spaces, plus two direct road interfaces and two future defensive wall joining sockets.

A separately engineered two-wide five-tread stone stair rises from the real southern guard area to the covered rampart; each step has physically placed bearing material under the tread and genuinely vacant headroom, then enters an upper landing supported by actual source deck. Four unequal tower masses rise directly from roof-bearing positions. Inert crates and barrels remain actual source voxels but confer no NPC or gameplay effects.

This is **not** Twinstone Pass relabeled: Twinstone's accepted-as-provisional source is a 28-long two-wide gate with twin bastions; Sunbarrow uses 34-long three-wide cart access, offset guard wings, two real absent-floor cooling courts, a distinct roof/stair route, and different physical masses. Final eight-way D4 numeric proof is still required.
