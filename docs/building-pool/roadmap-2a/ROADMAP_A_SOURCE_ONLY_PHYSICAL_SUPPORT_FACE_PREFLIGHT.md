## What the evaluator actually proves (and does not prove)

For a test asset requiring wall backing in the original producer, check its one correct adjacent support cell according to the unmodified producer's 4-yaw offset. For a ceramic floor tile, check the *cell directly below*. A neighbor is a **source-only full-face collider candidate** only when one exact, original physical collision AABB reaches the contacting unit-cell boundary and covers the entire one-cell transverse face. Do **not** extrapolate partial opaque coverage, round geometry into agreement, or combine disjoint collider fragments into a supposed full face. A neighbor with only thin plaster or thin tile collision is marked **partial/no certified full-face backing**. Empty target cells remain **not present in the explicitly supplied fixtures**.

Even the full-face label is **only a local collision-box candidate, never a bearing/structural certification or real world support**. No live world materials, caves, origin placement, physics loads, doors, building envelopes, renderer batches, actual GPU cost or runtime survival mechanics are resolved here. These checks do not prove that fired brick is a suitable final roof/floor/wall material; neither is a full-cube brick neighbor an automatic architectural choice.
