# Builder 2A M1 — honest source-cell roof-span preflight

The legacy roof audit counted flat roof cells without vertically underlying material but did not compute how far those cells are from real support. This owned read-only module now performs a **breadth-first roof-level flood from directly bearing wall/roof/floor cells** and reports per-level and per-building maximum lateral source-cell distances, disconnected roof fragments, missing raised-roof vertical roots, and flagged unverified masonry entrance/side spandrels. It checks all 44 authored buildings without changing geometry or runtime. A negative regression physically removes a wall bearing cell and requires an increase in unrooted roof plates. This is a conservative geometry diagnostic, **not a load calculation**: real shape meshing, span physics, material strengths, mounting glass, terrain and engineering remain unresolved for manual fill-in.
