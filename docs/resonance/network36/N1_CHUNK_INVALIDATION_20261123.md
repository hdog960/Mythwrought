# N1 chunk invalidation planner — source-only
Contract: `resonance-network36-chunk-invalidation-v1`. Pure `planChunkInvalidation` compares externally authenticated loaded-chunk snapshots with monotonic edit epochs, reports deterministic load/unload/edit changes, and refuses stale epochs or partial work above a 25-chunk/25-change budget. The caller must invalidate all affected topology and rederive graph membership before allocating any power; this module does **not** rebuild graphs or authenticate chunk state. Missing chunks are treated as unloads, not as an opportunity to bridge unloaded space. No writes to world, saves, UI, blocks, physics, or other owners. Focused command: `node --test tests/resonance-network36-chunk-invalidation.mjs`. Exact-head execution, authentic world adapters, iPhone/WebGL2 and offline acceptance remain outstanding.
