# Resonance generation24 candidate — G0/G1/G2
Version: `resonance-power-v1`. Isolated opt-in source, not production registered.
One unit is one Resonance unit per fixed 1000ms simulation tick. Capacity is a hard upper bound on output **per tick**, not storage. No transfer, storage, device demand or save writes are implemented.
`describeGenerator` yields deterministic anchored identity and bounded upgrade levels 0–3. `evaluateGenerator` returns availablePerTick, capacityPerTick, status, reason and fuelCostPerTick. `simulateTicks` accepts at most 60 ticks and reports accounting without mutating caller input.
Tuning candidates: hand 1/2, crystal 3/4, waterwheel 5/6, windmill 5/6, heat 7/8 (rated output/capacity). Each upgrade adds 1 rated output and 2 capacity. Crystal has no fuel cost but requires an externally verified crystal; rarity is metadata only. Waterwheel requires verified water movement and flow >=0.4; windmill verified exposure and wind >=0.3; heat requires verified heat and >=1 fuel unit per active tick. Inputs are opt-in *claims* supplied by a future authoritative adapter, not measured world conditions. Reject unsupported sources. No live biome/weather inference.
**Safety:** Caller must supply authoritative environment readings and validated placement/ownership; this prototype does not authenticate inputs. No power-network registration, fuel inventory withdrawal, persistent state, renderer, worldgen, or production hooks. :36 may consume immutable availability descriptors; :48 may consume rated output only through a future manual integrator. Tick accounting does not guarantee persisted fuel consumption. Never integrate without atomic fuel authorization and exact-source compatibility.
Run focused tests: `node --test tests/resonance-generation24-core.mjs`. Browser/WebGL2 evidence and real iPhone validation pending.
