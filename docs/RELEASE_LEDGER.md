## v0.10 Gate A automatic WebGL restore and original cave-wall rune checkpoint — OPEN
- [PR #29](https://github.com/hdog960/Mythwrought/pull/29) merged on `main` at `fefc610701ead6b91c78b02c61757c3a64425381`. A reusable `src/gpu-pipeline.mjs` reconstructs the original terrain and sky shaders, required attributes/uniforms, outline-buffer and viewport after a browser-driven `webglcontextrestored` event. `webglcontextlost` pauses the one-request animation driver, clears held inputs and displays a non-destructive wait notice. On restoration, only stale graphics/chunk caches are reset, then the original **same seed, live edited world and original schema-1 data** stream back into the same 5×5 visible chunk window without reloading or overwriting primary/journal, backup, vault or mirror. If shader recovery fails, rendering stays paused with a safe-reload notice rather than using half-valid GPU resources or falsely claiming recovery.
- Adds original deterministic **tree-and-star motifs** in warm gold or turquoise on sparse naturally open cave rock. A maximum of eight glyphs × ten stroked lines (60 vertices per glyph) per 16×16 chunk is rendered in the **same existing terrain GPU buffer and draw pass**. The two new shader materials, IDs 13–14, are renderer-only: no collision, new physical block, edits, dynamic light, combat/magic, permanent world content or save change. Removing a naturally engraved face or filling its open pocket removes the glyph; untouched natural walls regenerate the same one. This is small-scale **prototype artwork**, not the user's premium whimsical art approval or a magical city/castle.
- Initial [PR CI run 43148647909](https://github.com/hdog960/Mythwrought/actions/runs/43148647909) found that a rune integration assertion checked only four arbitrary chunks, none of which happened to contain runes. The test was corrected to inspect the game's entire bounded **5×5 visible window** while retaining strict mesh-integration and nonzero-geometry assertions. No older regression checks were weakened. Final PR candidate `291bde9` passed [124/124 Node plus all 15 Chromium suites](https://github.com/hdog960/Mythwrought/actions/runs/43148841889).
- **Authentic headless mobile Chromium screenshots were downloaded and inspected:** `mythwrought-v010-tree-star-cave-glyphs-before-loss.png`, `mythwrought-v010-context-restored-cycle-1.png`, `mythwrought-v010-context-restored-cycle-2.png`, and `mythwrought-v010-context-restored-grips.png`. This is **Chromium synthetic GPU loss**, not genuine Safari/iPhone OS memory eviction or art acceptance.
