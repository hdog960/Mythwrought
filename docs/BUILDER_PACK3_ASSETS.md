# Approved Pack 3 — physical builder-asset handoff

Creator approved the **17 Pack 3 roofing/monumental physical families** (symbolic rosters **pack3:023–029**, **pack3:069–078**) for future village, town and city building. The immutable source and all seven original candidate test suites were copied byte-for-byte from approved PR #112 at **3854612a6bca6cf57e63dde095e41eaac0ddfb8d**.

The physical builder adapter at `src/builder-assets/pack3.mjs` resolves those exact blueprint symbols to block IDs **109–125** and the real authored AABB material parts and supported yaw rotations. Building roadmap authors can call `resolvePack3BuilderAsset(symbol,orientation)` and `planPack3BuilderStructure(placements)` to obtain real, source-exact physical geometry without borrowing provisional 0–8 legacy IDs or making up block occupancy. The four roof-surface materials retain their certified north-only state; the other 13 families have all four quarter-turns.

**Important registration gate:** this is the safe physical *builder-source* incorporation, not yet live game registration. The current `VoxelWorld.set` only accepts block IDs 0–8; `meshChunk`, depth rendering, player picking/collision, palette selection and schema-1 save/rotation persistence are not yet integrated with new 109–125 IDs. Do not mark these assets as live-placeable or deploy them in world generation before a separate proven engine bridge and save-compatible validation. A future building dependency fill-in phase can use these definitions now; live settlements remain gated.
