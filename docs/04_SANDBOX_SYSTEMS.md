# Authority 04 — Voxel sandbox, magic engineering, ecology and settlements
**Status:** target sandbox contract.

## World edits, gravity and fluids
All normal terrain can be mined, placed, shaped and retained; floating fortresses, impossible spans and hollowed mountains remain. Falling physics ONLY for designated sand/gravel-like blocks; NO general structural integrity. Fluid choice B: block-based water/lava flows, irrigation, controlled drainage, waterfalls, freezing/thawing, heat and magic interactions, limited pumping and gates. No expensive realistic pressure or full distant flood simulation. Wet conductive spells and environment combinations follow coherent rules. Fire spread/world damage configurable; default protect user builds from incidental enemy/world damage. Manual terrain edits remain broadly free, protected automation respects zones.

## Building & blueprints
Original modular fantasy blocks: roofs, arches, carved supports, railings, windows, doors, furniture, trim, cultural/regional sets. Basic block building at start. Capture any valid existing structure as blueprint AND use a dedicated 3D design editor with virtual unrestricted palette, rotate/mirror/layer/edit/undo, bill of materials, overlays and conflict warnings. Save/export/import designs as versioned data; never silently overwrite world or consume unsupplied resources. Build manually, through explicit authorized companion construction, or via permanent material-consuming construction spells.

## Mining/geology
Familiar Minecraft-style ore deposits by depth and biome; NO elaborate geological prospector simulation. Manual mining, magic digging and companion-assisted mining. Automated runic drill/quarry ONLY excavates genuine blocks in explicit bounded player 3D work zones, consumes energy, respects story/resident/construction/sanctuary protection and halts on unsafe fluids, occupied habitats or unrecognized ancient artifacts. No self-expanding excavation. Real materials to storage. Consistent processing for unloaded chunks without free ore. Mining can expose EXISTING pre-generated caves/dungeons/cities, not summon loot to reward machine use.

## Modular magical engineering
Four high-level families: energy sources (crystal, waterwheel, contained lava heat as compatible), sensors/triggers, control/logic runes (switch, timer, condition, routing), outputs/devices (lamp, gate, pump, lift, machine). Distinguish energy from signals and items. HYBRID wiring: visible rune conduits, concealed conduit-compatible architecture, limited-range advanced paired wireless relay crystals. Relay NOT travel anchor. Runic Sight overlays hidden conduits, energy/status and errors, only when activated. Substantial but manageable optional automation: storage networks, sorting, transport, scheduled recipe crafting, smelting, farming/harvesting, alchemy, workshops, water and light mechanisms, protective wards and powered lifts. A machine never creates items without valid inputs/energy. Bounded throughput, cycle detection and update budgets; no elaborate general-purpose programmable factory simulator. Blueprints preserve device configurations and interconnections, but not free resources.

## Farming and cozy life
Minecraft farming freedom with Stardew-like optional farming, seasonal/regional crops, orchard, livestock/pets/mounts, fishing minigame, aquarium, cooking with varied recipes/bonuses/gifting, alchemy and magical crops. No constant upkeep or oppressive hunger meter. Structures/roads/bridges/farms recognized where functional.

## Pets, mounts and distinctive city pet shops — discussion direction (2026-11-11)
**Keep this simple and charming.** The player wants **adorable pets and rideable mounts**, primarily for companionship, personality and enjoyable travel. Some individual pets or mounts may provide **modest optional help in combat**, but pets should not become a complicated combat-build, creature-breeding, management or upkeep system.

**Major cities can each have unique pet shops**, with a small memorable selection reflecting the city's culture, location and ecology rather than every shop selling identical animals. Cute companions can be named, follow the player, and spend time around a home or settlement. Mounts can carry the player through the editable world, with navigation and controls kept practical. Optional finding, befriending or rescue in the wild may complement shops without becoming mandatory collection gameplay.

Maintain the existing promise of no forced feeding/cleaning chores or mandatory creature collection. This is a **design note, not an implementation order**; no pet species list, shop inventory, mount mechanics, artwork approval or release milestone is finalized by the discussion.

## Settlements and trade
Build optional living settlements, recognized by flexible functional home/workstation detection (entrances, furnishings, valid path). Up to modest active local NPC count within performance budget; distant abstract simulation. NPC jobs, visits, social spaces, trade, local economy, relations, routes and permission rules. Surface/deep connections by physically passable, safe, agreed-upon routes; trade only after conditions met. No forced mayor simulator. Terraria-like unique service resident acquisition combines milestones and world discovery with normal immigration; not every NPC an authored companion.

## Underground ecology
Light changes predator/prey/stealth/settlement safety. Some avoid light, others drawn by it, blind vibration/sound species may ignore it. Near-black expanses, luminous biomes, inhabited deep cities. Wildlife behaves according to environment, not ubiquitous aggression. Rare burrowers may open small persistent tunnels only in eligible unprotected natural blocks, never player-built structures or story sites; avoid fluid disasters. Nearby living creatures fully simulated; far-away habitats recorded in summary form. Animals organically choose habitats based on water, flora, light, noise, shelter and accessible paths. Selective domestication, trust and optional humane rescue/relocation for appropriate wildlife only, not universal capture.

## Sanctuary system
Wildlife habitat operates organically EVERYWHERE. Optional named protected sanctuary with boundary, atlas pin (NOT automatic fast travel), species observation and explicit automated-work restrictions. Designation never auto-spawns animals. Player may assign caretaker for observation, approved plants, equipment maintenance and wildlife monitoring; never unilateral relocation/capture, rare-material spending or excavation. Sanctuaries and ordinary settlements can coexist. Field journal unlocks observations, not collection grind.
